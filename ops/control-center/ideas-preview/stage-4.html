What already exists?
WHAT WE THINK OF IT
We don’t know, and here is why. This is our own operating console; nobody outside this company chooses between it and something else, so what else exists cannot change what we recommend — that is why the depth is Light and we did not look. Two honest cautions rather than a comfortable answer: that is not evidence that nothing comparable exists, and no agent in this company can browse the web, so we could not have checked even if it mattered.

We do not yet see a strong differentiation — and at this depth we did not look for one, because there is no market to be different in. Inside the company there is one real difference worth naming: today’s screens report status, and this one would show movement.

At Light depth this section is not produced as a market claim, for the same reason section 5 is not: differentiation is a comparison, and there is nothing to compare against that anyone chooses between. 06
What could make it fail?
WHAT WE THINK OF IT
The data. task_steps exists on 1 of 24 tasks, so there is no honest percentage; project_id is NULL on 20 of 24, so “which app is this build for” is unanswerable for most work; and no pipeline agent writes an agent_runs row, so there is no live signal to animate. A loop with nothing moving on it is worse than the list it replaced. Second: committing to the ellipse now fixes the least certain part of the design before we know what there is to put on it.

TECHNICAL
The records cannot support the picture. Changes how we build it: the first version has to render absent data as absent, not animate a placeholder over it.

COMPETITIVE, MARKET, BUSINESS, REGULATORY — NOT PRODUCED
Depth is Light. None of these could change whether or how we build an internal console, and a threat that would not change the decision does not belong on this list. This section is deliberately short: it is not a risk register.
07
What does the company recommend?
WE RECOMMEND
Proceed with narrowed scope: build the one-glance screen, and do not commit to the ellipse yet. First, one screen whose subject is the build — what the factory is making, which of the six gates it has passed, whose turn it is, what is stopping it — honest about what the records cannot support. Postponed on purpose: motion, percentages, and the ring geometry itself, until one real build has run through and we know what actually moves.

Expanded · sections 9, 10 and 12
9 · WHY THIS AND NOT SOMETHING ELSE
The outcome you named is “understand within seconds”. That is a legibility requirement, not a geometry requirement. Committing the shape in the brief would freeze the least certain decision at the moment we know least. One recommendation, not five options: build the screen, test the ring against alternatives during design, pick on evidence.
10 · IN SCOPE NOW
One Build screen · the six-stage gate ladder instead of a percentage · last event and how long ago · whose turn it is · what is stuck and why · the never-run state as a designed state · a Team page · every existing register still reachable, Meetings in at most two clicks
10 · NOT IN THE FIRST VERSION
The ellipse geometry as a commitment · motion and live indicators (nothing writes them today) · any percentage · any dollar figure · deleting any existing destination · anything about the built app’s own content, users or quality
12 · ALTERNATIVES WORTH CONSIDERING — TWO
Fix the navigation instead of the screen. What triggered this was not finding Meetings in a thirteen-item bar. A shorter bar and one grouped menu is a fraction of the work. We do not recommend it as the whole answer — it does not turn reading into looking — but it is worth doing either way, and it is cheap.
Wait for one real end-to-end build first. The screen designs itself once something has actually run through it. Cost: delay. Benefit: no screen built around imagined activity.
Two, not three. We are not adding a third to look thorough.
08
What assumptions did the company make?
WHAT WE THINK OF IT
Two that could change the answer. That “as simple as a dashboard” means fewer things on one screen, not fewer capabilities behind it — so today’s registers move behind one menu rather than being deleted. And that “track flow” means work moving through our own gates, with a build as the unit on the screen, not an agent. Both are correctable in one click if we have them wrong.

Expanded · section 11 · important assumptions
Simplify means consolidate, not delete. If you meant delete, the scope shrinks and several records stop being reachable — a materially different brief.
The unit on the screen is a build, not an agent. If you want to watch agents rather than work, this is a different screen with different data behind it.
You will keep using this yourself. One user, one machine. No multi-user, no permissions, no sharing was designed for. If that is wrong, say so before we build.
Three, and no more. Assumptions that could not change the result — font choice, page title, where the menu sits — are ours to make and are not listed here.
09
What decisions do you need from me?
WE NEED FROM YOU
Two. Both would produce a different brief depending on how you answer, which is the only reason either is here. You can approve without answering them — if you do, we proceed on the assumptions above and the brief will say so.
1 — Is the subject of this screen the factory, or the app the factory builds?
What changes: the factory, and the screen shows stages, owners and stalls, and we can build it now. The app being built, and the screen has almost nothing to show — projects holds one row, this ops system itself, so the factory has never built an app.
2 — Is the ellipse a requirement, or your sketch of one?
What changes: a sketch, and Design tests the ring against two or three other forms and picks on evidence. A requirement, and we build the ring first and accept that there may be very little to put on it.

Five candidate questions were written and three were deleted, because two honest answers to them produce the same brief: which colours to use, whether the menu sits left or top, and whether the Team page ships in the same release. 10
How will we know we succeeded?
WE RECOMMEND
You open one screen and, without clicking, can say what the factory is making, which of the six gates it has passed, whose turn it is, and what is stopping it. Nothing on the screen is a number the records cannot support. A factory that has never run renders as a designed state, not an empty grid. And Meetings — the page you could not find — is reachable in at most two clicks.

None we can see, and we did not look — there is no market here. The one difference that matters internally: today’s screens report status, this one would show movement.
