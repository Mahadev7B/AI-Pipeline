Why there is no progress bar here
There is no honest number to put in one. Positions are gathered at the same time, not in sequence, and this product records nothing while a run is in flight — 0 of 13 recorded agent runs carries a duration or a cost. A bar here would be an animation, not a measurement. There is no estimated finish time for the same reason.
You will not be shown three reports. One answer comes back, and it will say who was consulted.
