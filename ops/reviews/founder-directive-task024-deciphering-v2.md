TASK-024 FOUNDER SCOPE CORRECTION — PART 1 OF 3
IDEA DECIPHERING + COMPANY PERSPECTIVES

We are simplifying and strengthening the idea-understanding stage.

Do NOT build a special "brainstorming" feature.
Do NOT depend on a brainstorming skill.
Do NOT make "Brainstorm More" a primary product concept.

The important requirement is this:

When the Founder gives the AI Factory a raw idea, the factory must return a high-quality, structured interpretation and evaluation of that idea BEFORE any downstream build work begins.

The factory is responsible for understanding Founder intent, evaluating the idea intelligently, identifying competitors and threats where relevant, and recommending what should actually be built.

The Founder should NOT have to provide a polished product specification.

==================================================
1. CORE JOURNEY
==================================================

RAW IDEA
    ↓
FACTORY INTERPRETS / DECIPHERS
    ↓
FACTORY EVALUATES IDEA
    ↓
FOUNDER REVIEWS
    ↓
FOUNDER CORRECTS OR APPROVES
    ↓
FOUNDER-APPROVED PRODUCT BRIEF
    ↓
START WORK
    ↓
DOWNSTREAM COMPANY EXECUTION

Do not start Design, CTO, Red Team, Development, or downstream execution before the Founder approves the interpreted brief.

==================================================
2. RAW IDEA
==================================================

Founder may enter an idea in ordinary language.

It may be:

- short
- incomplete
- informal
- uncertain
- conversational
- poorly structured
- missing business details
- missing UX details
- missing technical details

That is acceptable.

The Founder should NOT have to write a PRD.

Preserve the Founder's original wording exactly.

Never overwrite the raw idea.

==================================================
3. IDEA DECIPHERING
==================================================

The factory must determine:

- What does the Founder appear to mean?
- What problem or opportunity is behind the idea?
- What outcome is the Founder actually trying to achieve?
- Who would use this?
- What value would it provide?
- What should probably be built?
- What belongs in the first useful version?
- What important assumptions are we making?
- Are there materially better or simpler approaches?
- Does something similar already exist?
- Who are the known competitors or substitutes?
- What could threaten the idea?
- What makes the idea potentially valuable?
- What genuinely requires a Founder decision?

This is NOT simply rewriting the Founder's sentence.

==================================================
4. INTERNAL COMPANY PERSPECTIVES
==================================================

Chief of Staff may consult relevant company agents internally.

PRODUCT

Focus on:
- problem
- target user
- product outcome
- scope
- requirements
- alternatives
- product assumptions

CEO

Focus on:
- strategic value
- market direction
- whether we are solving the right problem
- business opportunity
- differentiation
- positioning
- whether the idea deserves company resources

CTO

Focus on:
- technical feasibility
- major technical constraints
- simpler technical approaches
- architecture implications
- dependencies
- expensive-to-reverse decisions

RED TEAM

Focus on:
- weak assumptions
- reasons the idea could fail
- misunderstood Founder intent
- unnecessary complexity
- simpler alternatives
- competitive weaknesses
- hidden dependencies
- reasons users might not care

DESIGN

Use when UX materially affects the idea.

Focus on:
- user experience
- interaction model
- usability
- user journey
- whether the proposed experience solves the problem

FINANCIAL

Use when relevant.

Focus on:
- likely cost structure
- pricing possibilities
- economic feasibility
- expensive dependencies
- potential margins

SECURITY / PRIVACY

Use only when genuinely relevant.

Focus on:
- identity
- payments
- sensitive data
- permissions
- trust boundaries
- privacy expectations
- material regulatory constraints

Do NOT automatically involve every agent.

Chief of Staff should select only perspectives that can materially improve the interpretation.

==================================================
5. DECIPHER BROADLY, COMMUNICATE NARROWLY
==================================================

The Founder must NOT receive separate reports from each agent.

Agents may disagree internally.

Chief of Staff must synthesize everything into ONE Founder-facing result.

The Founder experience should feel like:

"I gave my company an idea.
My leadership team considered it.
Now they are telling me what they think I mean, whether it looks promising, what exists already, what could go wrong, and what they recommend."

Not:

"Here are seven reports. Figure it out yourself."

==================================================
6. FOUNDER QUESTIONS
==================================================

Ask the Founder only questions that materially affect:

- product direction
- target market
- major scope
- business intent
- meaningful UX direction
- irreversible choices
- significant cost
- external commitments
- monetization
- other genuine Founder decisions

Do NOT ask questions that Product, CEO, CTO, Design, or Developer can reasonably decide themselves.

Zero questions is acceptable.

One or two important questions is preferable to eight minor questions.

TASK-024 FOUNDER SCOPE CORRECTION — PART 3 OF 3
FOUNDER APPROVAL + DOWNSTREAM SOURCE OF TRUTH + UI

Continue from Parts 1 and 2.

==================================================
1. FOUNDER-FACING SUMMARY
==================================================

The deciphering output must be quick to understand.

The Founder should be able to answer:

1. Did the factory understand my idea?
2. What am I really trying to achieve?
3. Why might this be worth building?
4. What already exists?
5. What could make ours different?
6. What could make it fail?
7. What does the company recommend?
8. What assumptions did the company make?
9. What decisions does the company actually need from me?
10. How will we know we succeeded?

Use progressive disclosure.

The primary Founder view should remain concise.

Detailed competitor analysis, technical concerns, assumptions, and evidence can be expanded when wanted.

Do not force the Founder to read a giant consulting report.

==================================================
2. FOUNDER REVIEW
==================================================

After the factory produces the interpretation, the Founder reviews it.

Provide clear actions such as:

[ Edit / Correct ]

[ Reconsider ]

[ Approve Brief ]

"Reconsider" means:

The Founder provides feedback and the company re-evaluates its interpretation, assumptions, recommendation, or scope.

Do NOT make "Brainstorm More" the main terminology.

The objective is:

CORRECT UNDERSTANDING

not endless ideation.

==================================================
3. FOUNDER-APPROVED PRODUCT BRIEF
==================================================

Once the Founder approves the interpretation, create a durable:

FOUNDER-APPROVED PRODUCT BRIEF

Preserve all three separately:

RAW FOUNDER IDEA
        ↓
FACTORY-INTERPRETED / EVALUATED BRIEF
        ↓
FOUNDER-APPROVED PRODUCT BRIEF

Never overwrite one stage with another.

The company must always be able to answer:

- What did the Founder originally say?
- What did the company interpret?
- What merits did the company identify?
- What competitors did it evaluate?
- What threats did it identify?
- What did it recommend?
- What assumptions did it make?
- What did the Founder ultimately approve?

==================================================
4. DOWNSTREAM SOURCE OF TRUTH
==================================================

THIS IS A HARD REQUIREMENT.

Once a Founder-approved brief exists:

Product,
Design,
CTO,
Red Team,
Developer,
Code Review,
QA,
Security,
and other downstream agents

must receive the FOUNDER-APPROVED PRODUCT BRIEF as the authoritative product instruction.

Do NOT continue passing the raw Founder idea as the primary downstream prompt.

The raw idea remains available for historical context only.

Every downstream agent should work from the same approved understanding.

Do not allow each agent to independently reinterpret the original one-line idea.

==================================================
5. START WORK
==================================================

Founder approval of the brief and starting execution are related but consequential.

The Founder must deliberately authorize execution.

Use a clear action such as:

[ Approve Brief & Start Work ]

Before execution starts, communicate that:

- AI agents will begin working
- real AI cost may be incurred
- the approved brief becomes the authoritative instruction

Do NOT auto-start execution merely because the raw idea was saved.

==================================================
6. REQUIRED UI CHANGES
==================================================

THIS IS A FOUNDER-FACING UX CHANGE, NOT ONLY A BACKEND WORKFLOW CHANGE.

Update the TASK-024 Design.

The complete journey must be visually demonstrated:

RAW IDEA
    ↓
FACTORY INTERPRETING
    ↓
FACTORY UNDERSTANDING
    ↓
IDEA EVALUATION
    ↓
FOUNDER REVIEW
    ↓
CORRECTION / RECONSIDERATION
    ↓
FOUNDER APPROVAL
    ↓
APPROVED BRIEF
    ↓
START WORK
    ↓
FACTORY BEGINS EXECUTION

The UI must make it easy to distinguish:

WHAT I SAID

WHAT THE FACTORY THINKS I MEANT

WHAT THE FACTORY THINKS ABOUT THE IDEA

WHAT THE FACTORY RECOMMENDS

WHAT I APPROVED

==================================================
7. WHAT THE UI SHOULD SHOW
==================================================

The interpreted-idea view should visibly include, where relevant:

- Original Idea
- What We Think You Mean
- What You Are Really Trying to Achieve
- Why This May Be Valuable
- Known Competitors / Alternatives
- Potential Differentiation
- Threats / Weaknesses
- Recommended Direction
- Initial Scope
- Important Assumptions
- Founder Decisions Needed
- Definition of Success
- Company View

Do NOT display all of this as one giant wall of text.

Use a concise executive summary first.

Allow deeper sections to expand.

The experience should feel like receiving an executive recommendation from your own AI company.

Not like reading an internal project-management document.

==================================================
8. AGENT INVOLVEMENT IN THE UI
==================================================

The Founder does not need to read each agent's raw output.

The UI may show something lightweight such as:

Company perspectives consulted:
Product · CEO · CTO · Red Team

But Chief of Staff presents ONE synthesized company recommendation.

Do not expose internal transcripts by default.

==================================================
9. WHAT NOT TO BUILD
==================================================

Do NOT:

- build a standalone brainstorming subsystem
- invent a brainstorming skill dependency
- automatically involve every agent
- dump agent reports on the Founder
- ask generic discovery questions
- make the Founder write a PRD
- auto-start from the raw idea
- let downstream agents use different interpretations
- invent competitor information
- create fake market numbers
- create fake progress
- create endless idea-analysis loops

==================================================
10. DEPTH SHOULD SCALE WITH THE IDEA
==================================================

Do not treat every idea as a venture-capital due-diligence exercise.

Example:

A small internal utility may need:

- interpretation
- scope
- assumptions
- recommendation

and almost no competitor research.

A new commercial SaaS product may need:

- current competitor research
- substitutes
- differentiation
- business threats
- pricing/cost considerations
- CEO / Financial / Red Team perspectives

Chief of Staff should determine the appropriate depth.

==================================================
11. PROCESS ORDER
==================================================

Record this as the latest Founder correction to TASK-024.

Product must revise the brief first.

Then Design must revise the full Founder journey.

Before Development begins, show the Founder the revised visual/clickable mockup.

Do NOT proceed to implementation merely because the workflow document is correct.

The Founder-facing UX must also make sense.

==================================================
FINAL PRINCIPLE
==================================================

The factory's job is not merely to say:

"I can build what you typed."

The factory should be able to say:

"Here is what we believe you mean.

Here is what you are really trying to achieve.

Here is why this may be worth building.

Here is what already exists.

Here is what could make ours different.

Here is what could make it fail.

Here is what we recommend.

Here are the assumptions we made.

Here is what we genuinely need from you.

Here is how we will know we succeeded."

The Founder reviews and approves that understanding.

ONLY THEN does the company build.
