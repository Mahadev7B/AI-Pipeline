About this prototype
Working navigation, no backend. Every control here moves you between drawn states; nothing is dispatched, no model is invoked and no money is spent by clicking anything. The idea it walks is real — the Founder’s own words for TASK-026, stored in this database, together with the real feedback given later. The database facts quoted throughout (0 of 13 agent runs carrying a cost, task_steps on 1 of 24 tasks, project_id NULL on 20 of 24, one row in projects) are real. Anything the product could not know honestly is drawn as a bracketed placeholder rather than filled in.
