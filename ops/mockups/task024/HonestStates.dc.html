Drawn, not described
The honest states
Every one of these is a state the product will actually be in, and most of them are states a product is tempted to skip drawing. Where the tempting wrong version is instructive, it is shown beside the right one and marked.
Honest — no knowledge of competitors
We are not aware of established competitors, and cannot check. That is not evidence there are none.
Absence of knowledge, stated as absence of knowledge.
Never — the flattering fabrication
There are no competitors. This is a clear space.
Rendering “we do not know” as “there are none” is a failure every time. It is the sentence the Founder most wants to hear, which is exactly why it needs a rule and not a judgement call.
Honest — an unanswerable question, answered in the concise layer
04 · What already exists?
We don’t know, and here is why. Nobody outside this company chooses between this screen and something else, so what exists cannot change the recommendation — that is why depth is Light and we did not look. Also: no agent here can browse the web, so we could not have checked.
The ten questions are a completeness contract. An uncomfortable answer is still an answer, and it stays in the concise layer — the one place a Founder is guaranteed to read.
Honest — no differentiation
05 · What could make ours different?
We do not yet see a strong differentiation, and at this depth we did not look for one. Inside the company there is one real difference: today’s screens report status, this one would show movement.
A legitimate, visible answer — not a blank, not a hedge, and not a claim dressed up to fill the space.
Honest — an empty section that says why
5 · KNOWN COMPETITORS
NOT PRODUCED
Depth is Light. Nobody outside chooses between this and an alternative, so competitor analysis could not change the recommendation. Producing it would be research theatre.
A competitor section at Light depth is a defect, not diligence. An empty section with a reason is a decision; an empty section without one is a bug.
Honest — zero questions
We have nothing to ask you.
Everything we were unsure about could be stated as an assumption you can correct in one click, so we did that instead of making you answer questions.
Zero is a passing score. Eight small questions is the failure — and if we ever ask you eight, the right response is to distrust the round.
Honest — the start failed
Not started. TASK-026 is still in the Backlog.
No agent was started, nothing was spent, the status is unchanged, the approved brief is untouched. Try again — trying again starts one pipeline, not two.
The word “started” never renders unless a status transition was written. This project has already shipped one success message over a no-op; that is not a hypothetical risk here.
Honest — no cost estimate exists
This product cannot tell you what this will cost. 0 of the 13 agent runs on record carries a cost figure, so there is no honest estimate to show. Watch it on Costs as it accrues.
[ enforced per-round ceiling — computed from the real dispatch path; not yet wired ]
There is an enforced ceiling, and the honest thing is to show the slot empty rather than copy a figure from a document. A fabricated number with “roughly” in front of it is still fabricated.
Honest — no fake progress
There is no progress bar here because there is no honest number to put in one. Positions are gathered at the same time, not in sequence, and nothing is recorded while a run is in flight.
The bar above is what this product will not ship: an animation reporting a fact it does not have. No percentage, no estimated finish, no spinner pretending to measure.
Honest — a roster of one
Product
Only Product was consulted. Nobody else could name a question about this idea that would have changed the answer, so nobody else was paid to look at it.
Legitimate and expected — but never silent. A roster of one is rendered as a roster of one with its reason, not as a blank space. And a Product-only round that comes back with a long assumption list is evidence the roster was too narrow; the result is expected to say so.
Honest — a perspective we could not consult
Design would have been material here — this is a user-experience idea. It could not be consulted: design is not on the meeting participant allowlist today.
A missing perspective that is named is a known gap. A missing perspective that is not named is an unknown one, and the Founder has no way to discover it.
Honest — nothing saved yet
No ideas saved yet. Anything you save appears here and stays here until you start it.
The first-run state and the standing ledger are the same element. A parked idea is never presented as a failure or a stall — it is waiting on the Founder, and the screen says that in those words.
The rule underneath all ten
An empty space with a reason is information. An empty space without one is a bug. A filled space without evidence is a lie. Every state on this sheet is the first or second kind, and the third kind — a number the records cannot support, a competitor nobody checked, a progress bar measuring nothing, a “started” over a failed dispatch — does not ship, at any depth, with or without a hedge in front of it.
